## TL;DR (decision-relevant)

1. **Ranking is the bottleneck, not coverage.** In a pre-explored graph, the R2R-construction prior (goal = a node whose weighted shortest path from the start has 4–6 edges and is ≥5 m long) gives about **22 candidates per episode (median 19)** on val_unseen and about **23** (median 20) for the R2R test starts. That set covers **98.6 %** of GT goals. Because **99–100 % of R2R GT paths are exact graph shortest paths**, it also contains the exact GT path. Picking a candidate at random succeeds 22.8 % of the time (about 4 candidates lie within 3 m of the goal). Older beam searches had final-node oracles of 90 % (FAST, K=20) and union-OSR of 96–99 % (30–40 beams), but top-1 after reranking was only 54–78 %.
2. **Historical value of search and rerank over single-run: +11 to +19 SR** (Speaker-Follower 35.5→54.6 val-unseen; RCM 43→63, EnvDrop 51→69, AuxRN 55→71, Active-VLN 60→71 on test). **Pre-exploration (self-training on unlabeled test scenes) added +10 to +18 SR** (RCM 43→61, EnvDrop 51→64, AuxRN 55→68, Active-VLN 60→70 test). Our setting allows *both*, plus a complete map, and the Score has no SPL term.
3. **Ensembling over the 3 instructions of a trajectory is worth about +6–7 SR (measured).** I used the released Airbert and VLN-BERT predictions and a 3 m-ball/medoid vote over the 3 instructions that share a path. Airbert val_unseen goes **68.7 → 75.7**, VLN-BERT **59.3 → 65.1**, and 2 models × 3 instructions give 76.9. The any-of-3 oracle is 90.8. **Agreement is a well-calibrated confidence signal:** when all 3 agree (51 % of paths) accuracy is **89.7 %**; with 2/3 it is 69.0 %; with no agreement 34.8 %.
4. **Marginal values.** (a) Trained compatibility, follower and speaker models are the primary engine. In-domain pretraining moved a ranker from 59.3 to 68.7 on its own, ensembles reached 73–78 on test, and modern single-run agents reach 80–85 on test. (b) A frontier LLM/VLM used zero-shot as a navigator sits about 30+ points below supervised models (MapGPT GPT-4V 47.7 SR on a subset). It is best used as an **instruction parser and tie-breaker on low-agreement trajectories**, not as the main ranker. (c) A speaker likelihood is weak alone (43.7 % in FAST) but is the most valuable *complementary* signal (+10.9 SR in Speaker-Follower; weight β = 0.94 against the follower in VLN-BERT's fusion).
5. **Expected top-1 goal accuracy within 3 m (per test trajectory), inference:**
   - With HA-R2R instructions only and a fine-tuned modern ranker ensemble plus 3-instruction fusion: **≈ 85 % (plausible range 78–90 %)**.
   - If the 3 original R2R test instructions can legitimately be matched in (R2R-CE test `trajectory_id` = R2R `path_id`): **≈ 88 % (83–92 %)**.
   - Practical ceiling: about 92–95 % (R2R annotation noise; the human follower reaches 86 % SR).
   - Expect test ≈ val_unseen(R2R-CE subset) − 1…3 points, with a ±4-point scan-level CI.
   - With TCR = 0, each +1 point of SR is worth about **+0.9 Score**.
6. **HA-R2R specific.** HA-R2R instructions were generated by GPT-4o / LLaMA-3 from the **original R2R-CE instruction plus `region: activity` human annotations at [start]/[during]/[end]**. Those annotations exist per viewpoint for **all 18 test scans** in `human_motion_text.json` (70 humans, activity strings unique within each scan). That makes human mentions potentially strong goal anchors (validate on val_unseen). One limit: VLN-BERT truncates at **60 tokens** and Rec-VLN-BERT at 80, while HA-R2R needs about 140–170 tokens.

## 1. Settings taxonomy and how ours maps

| Setting (R2R leaderboard era) | What is allowed | Typical metric effect | Our Track-2 situation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single run | Online, greedy, no prior knowledge of the test scene | SR, SPL | Not binding: scoring replays our submitted actions |
| **Pre-explore** | Agent may explore test scenes **without instructions** before evaluation (e.g., RCM's SIL, EnvDrop back-translation on test scenes) | SR +10–18 over single run; SPL stays high | We have the full MP3D mesh, navmesh and connectivity graph of the 18 test scans offline → superset |
| **Beam search** | Many trajectories per instruction, pick the best; leaderboard **charges the full exploration path** (SPL ≈ 0.01) | SR +11–19; SPL ≈ 0 | Exploration happens offline; we submit only the chosen path → beam-search SR **without** an SPL penalty, and Score has no SPL term |
| **Path selection / "pre-explored environment" (VLN-BERT, Airbert)** | Rank N=30 candidate trajectories from a beam search; metrics on the selected path | 73–78 test SR | Exactly our goal-ranking problem, except our candidates can be **exhaustive** (all prior-plausible nodes with their shortest paths) |

Airbert states this mapping explicitly: *"as the discriminative model uses path selection for R2R, we follow the pre-explored environment setting adopted by VLN-BERT, and compute metrics on the selected path"* (`ext/b4/airbert-vln_airbert-vln.github.io/paper.html`, R2R Setup paragraph). Confidence: high.

In addition, collisions count only on the **executed** path (C7/A2 territory), and SR additionally requires TCR_ep == 0.

### 4.4 Ranker implementation facts (code)

- **VLN-BERT** (`arjunmajum_vln-bert/utils/cli.py`, `beam_dataset.py`, `scripts/combine-models.py`):
  - Inputs: `max_instruction_length` = **60** tokens, `max_path_length` = 8 nodes, 101 regions per pano (bottom-up Faster R-CNN features, 13.8 GB LMDB).
  - Candidates: 30 beams from EnvDrop.
  - Training: 1 positive (final node ≤3 m) vs 3 negatives.
  - Fusion: `alpha·scale·vlnbert + (1−alpha)·(beta·speaker + (1−beta)·follower)` with α = 0.63, β = 0.94, scale = 0.01.
- **Speaker-Follower** (`rational_follower.py` L131–144): speaker weight 0.95, applied after dividing each score by its std.
- **Airbert:**
  - Same ViLBERT backbone; fine-tuning with "1 positive vs 3 negatives" plus 2 visually-shuffled negatives.
  - Augmentation: EnvDrop speaker data (178 k pairs).
  - BnB pretraining uses a 64-GPU SLURM script.
  - Weak spot: picking the correct instruction against left/right-switched corruptions is only **49.8 %** (val-unseen; VLN-BERT 45.3 %), versus 61–67 % for noun corruptions.

## 5. What the literature implies about the signals

- **Follower / discriminative compatibility (a):**
  - The main engine. In-domain pretraining (BnB) took a ranker from 59.3 to 68.7 by itself. Shuffled-path negatives add +2.7, hard negatives +2.4, keyword highlighting +2.1. Simply adding more random wrong trajectories does nothing (−0.15).
  - Today's discrete agents (ScaleVLN 80, SRDF ~85 test single-run) are far stronger proposers and scorers than the EnvDrop beams (51) used by VLN-BERT/Airbert.
  - Scoring every candidate's shortest path by teacher-forced log-likelihood under these agents is a zero-training upgrade.
- **Speaker / pragmatic (part of a):** weak alone (FAST 43.7), but the best complement: +10.9 SR in Speaker-Follower; β = 0.94 in VLN-BERT's fusion; part of every top ensemble (73 and 77 test).
- **Ensembles of heterogeneous scorers:** +3–5 over the best single scorer (FAST +5.3; VLN-BERT ensemble 73 test vs 59 val alone; Airbert ensemble 77 test vs 68.7 val alone; the speaker+follower ensemble is about 68 val).
- **Frontier LLM/VLM judge (b):**
  - No published result for listwise LLM ranking of complete candidate paths on R2R that I could verify.
  - Zero-shot LLM *navigators* are 30–45 points below supervised models (MapGPT 47.7 on a subset; NavGPT ≈34 [memory]).
  - Expected role: (i) parse the long HA-R2R instruction into R2R style, ordered landmarks, directions and human anchors; (ii) break ties among the top 2–3 clusters on trajectories where the trained models disagree. Those are the 2/3-support and no-agreement buckets: about 49 % of paths, at 69 % and 35 % accuracy.
  - A VLM tie-breaker that is right 60 % of the time on the disagreement buckets would add roughly +1–4 SR (inference).
- **Instruction ensembling (c):** measured +5.9 to +7.1 SR for 2020–21 rankers with a crude vote. Product-of-experts over full score vectors should do at least as well (inference). For a modern base around 80–85 % I expect +3–5 (errors become more correlated and there is less headroom).
- **Pre-exploration / self-training on test scenes:** +10–18 SR historically. For modern models trained on ScaleVLN's 1,200 scenes the visual domain gap is smaller, so I expect +2–5 (inference).

## 6. HA-R2R-specific considerations

- **Generation process** (`HA-VLN/Data/HA-R2R/README.md`): GPT-4o and LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct, few-shot. The input is the original R2R-CE `instruction_text` plus `[start]`, `[during]` and `[end]` fields of the form `region: activity`. For example, `end: "hallway: An individual cleaning or vacuuming."` is exactly the string format of `HASimulator/HA-DE/human-viewpoint_annotation/human_motion_text.json`. Consequences:
  - The original navigational content is embedded, so it can be recovered by LLM de-paraphrasing.
  - Human mentions are tied to annotated viewpoints, which makes them potential goal anchors, especially the `[end]` field.
- **Test-scan coverage of human annotations:** `human_motion_text.json` lists 70 humans across the 18 test scans (2–7 per scan), keyed by viewpoint id, with activity strings unique within each scan. HA-VLN 2.0 `human_motion.json` (72 non-test scans, 749 humans) retains 248 of the 304 HA-VLN-1.0 viewpoints (82 %). For test scans, therefore, the 1.0 file is a partial (about 80 %?) subset of the 2.0 human set. Whether the test instructions were generated from 1.0 or 2.0 placements is unknown; validate on val_unseen.
- **Length.** About 112 words is roughly 140–170 BERT word-pieces. VLN-BERT (60) and Rec-VLN-BERT/BEVBert (80) would drop most of the instruction, including the final stop description. Use 200-token models (DUET, ScaleVLN, MAGIC, NavGPT-2) or larger, or LLM-compressed R2R-style rewrites.
- **Style shift.** R2R-trained models lose 15–18 SR on heavily restyled instructions (GSA-R2R; see B1), and HA-VLN-DE baselines roughly halve. Fine-tuning on the 10,819 HA-R2R train instructions (aligned to R2R viewpoint paths via `trajectory_id`) plus de-paraphrasing should recover most of this (inference; must be measured).
- **Original R2R instructions:** the local R2R-CE `test.json.gz` (`ext/Discrete-Continuous-VLN/...`) has 3,408 episodes and 1,136 `trajectory_id`s equal to R2R `path_id`s, with the original instruction text. If HA-R2R test keeps `trajectory_id` (A1), each test trajectory gets **6 instructions**, 3 of them in-distribution for every R2R model. Whether this is allowed needs a C3 sign-off.

## 7. Expected top-1 goal accuracy within 3 m (per trajectory) — estimate

| Stage | Expected top-1 @3 m | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 2021-era ranker, 1 R2R instruction, R2R-CE subset | 66.9 % | measured (Airbert) |
| + 3-instruction vote | 74.4 % | measured |
| Modern follower scoring (ScaleVLN/SRDF teacher-forced) over R2R-prior candidates, 1 R2R instruction | 78–84 % | single-run test 80–85 minus ~2 for the R2R-CE subset, plus a small search gain (inference) |
| + heterogeneous ensemble (3+ agents, speaker, fine-tuned cross-encoder) | 81–87 % | +3–5 historical ensemble gain, shrinking at high base (inference) |
| + product-of-experts over 3 instructions + 3 m-ball MAP | 84–90 % | +3–5 (measured +7 at a 69 % base; inference) |
| Using HA-R2R instructions only (style penalty after fine-tuning: −2 to −6; human anchors +0 to +3) | **≈ 85 % (78–90 %)** | inference |
| With the 3 original R2R instructions added (6 total) | **≈ 88 % (83–92 %)** | inference |
| Ceiling | ≈ 92–95 % | annotation noise; human 86 %; any-of-3 oracle 90.8 % with weak rankers |

**Translation to Score** (CONTEXT formula; with TCR = 0 on every episode, Social = 1): Score = 100 × (0.8·SR + 0.2·3/(3+NE)).
- With success-NE ≈ 1.2 m and failure-NE ≈ 7 m: SR 0.75 → about 70.6; SR 0.85 → about 80; SR 0.90 → about 85.
- So **1 point of SR ≈ 0.9 Score**.
- Stop-point rule: maximize posterior mass within a 3 m ball (Bayes-optimal for the 0.8·SR term), and break ties by expected distance (for NE).

## 8. Concrete training and evaluation recipe for a path ranker (HA-R2R val_unseen → test prediction)

**Data alignment (day 0–1)**
1. HA-R2R `{train, val_seen, val_unseen}.json.gz`: group episodes into trajectory triples. Map each `trajectory_id` to its R2R `path_id` (verify: R2R-CE `trajectory_id` equals the R2R `path_id` in the local file). Recover the GT goal viewpoint and the GT viewpoint path.
2. For every trajectory, build the candidate set:
   - nodes reachable from the start node with weighted-shortest-path edge count 2–8 and length ≥ 3 m;
   - prior weight w(edges, length) = train histogram (4–6 edges ≈ 99 %);
   - store the Dijkstra path to each node.
   - Expected sizes: about 22 (strict prior) to about 45 (soft prior) per episode.
3. Labels: soft target p*(v) ∝ 1[d(v, goal) < 3 m], or ∝ exp(−d²/2σ²) with σ = 1.5 m. Keep raw distances for NE analysis.

**Scorers**
- **S1 Follower log-likelihood (zero training).** For ScaleVLN-H14+EnvEdit, SRDF, MAGIC-L/GOAT and NavGPT-2, run DUET-style teacher forcing along each candidate path (`feedback='teacher'` with `gt_path` replaced by the candidate path; `ScaleVLN/VLN-DUET/map_nav_src/r2r/agent.py` `_teacher_action_r4r`). Record the sum and mean log-prob plus the STOP log-prob at the end node. Also record greedy rollouts as votes. Input variants: the raw HA-R2R instruction (≤200 tokens), and an LLM-condensed R2R-style instruction.
- **S2 Cross-encoder ranker (fine-tuned).**
  - Initialisation: ScaleVLN/SRDF pretrained DUET encoder; instruction length 256–320 tokens; path = up to 8 nodes of heading-aligned view features.
  - Objective: listwise softmax CE over all candidates per instruction.
  - Negatives: all candidates; shuffled visual paths (Airbert +2.7); left/right-swapped and landmark-swapped instructions; human-activity-swapped instructions.
  - Data: HA-R2R train (10,819) + R2R train (14,039) + EnvDrop/PREVALENT augmentation + LLM HA-style rewrites of augmentation instructions. Rewrites use the HA-R2R prompt with train-scan human annotations.
  - Budget: about 1–3 days on 8×A100 (inference).
- **S3 Speaker / PMI score.** Fine-tune a 7–8B LLM (LoRA) to generate the HA-R2R instruction from a verbalized path:
  - .house region sequence, mpcat40 objects, turn directions computed from the shortest-path geometry, floor changes, humans passed with their `region:activity` text.
  - Score = log P(instr | path) − log P(instr) (removes length/genericity bias).
  - This mirrors the GPT-4o generation process.
- **S4 Geometric/linguistic consistency.** An LLM parses the instruction into an ordered list of (turn, landmark, region) items. Compute rule-based agreement with the candidate path's turn sequence and region sequence. This targets the rankers' left/right weakness.
- **S5 Human-anchor feature.** Match each instruction's human activities (start/during/end) to annotated humans of the scan (1.0 file for test scans; 2.0 if the bundle provides it). Features: distance from the candidate node to the `end` human's viewpoint, and whether the path passes the `during` humans.
- **S6 VLM judge.** Only for trajectories whose top-2 clusters are within a margin, or whose 3-instruction agreement is below 3/3. Listwise prompt over the top-3 clusters with rendered panoramas along each path, humans inserted.

**Fusion and decision**
- Log-linear or logistic-regression fusion of the S1–S6 features per (instruction, candidate). Train it on **leave-scans-out folds of train** (e.g., 6 folds of about 10 scans) and val_seen, **not** on val_unseen.
- Then do product-of-experts across the 3 (or 6) instructions: sum the per-instruction log-posteriors per candidate.
- Final choice: v* = argmax_v Σ_u p(u)·1[d(v,u) < 3 m]. Break ties by expected distance.

**Evaluation protocol**
- Primary metric: top-1@3 m per trajectory on HA-R2R val_unseen (613 trajectories, 11 scans), using graph distance and also navmesh geodesic (Habitat 0.1.7).
- Secondary: top-k recall, cluster accuracy, NE, ECE of P(success), and the selective-accuracy-vs-coverage curve (agreement buckets).
- Report a scan-level bootstrap CI (expect about ±4 points).
- Limit val_unseen looks: log every evaluation and freeze recipes before the final look.
- Sanity baselines to reproduce first: random from the prior set (≈ 23 %); Airbert-style 3-vote on R2R instructions (≈ 74 %).
- **Test prediction:** test ≈ val_unseen − 1…3 points (historical R2R val→test gaps: DUET 72→69, ScaleVLN 81→80, EnvDrop 52→51). 18 test scans versus 11 val scans gives similar scan variance.
- End-to-end check: one Phase-1 val_unseen submission scored by the official scorer to confirm goal selection, execution, collisions and Score.

**Decision rules**
- Invest first in S1 + PoE + 3 m-ball (no training; days).
- Add S2 if S1 is below 80 % on HA-R2R val_unseen.
- Add S3/S4/S5 if fusion gains are ≥ 1 point each on the leave-scans-out folds.
- Keep S6 only if it gains ≥ 1 point on the disagreement buckets.
- Pre-exploration self-training on test scans (sample 4–6-edge paths, generate HA-style instructions with S3, fine-tune S2) only after a C3 rules sign-off. Expected +2–5.
