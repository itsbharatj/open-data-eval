## Summary

Regime tags used throughout: [C] = compliant at inference (single run, the episode's own instruction, forward RGB-D + GPS/compass, unlimited per-step compute, every observation paid for by actions inside the submitted tape); [C-train] = privileged information used only for training (allowed by draft FAQ Q11); [G] = gray, needs an explicit organizer ruling (sibling instructions of the same trajectory, multi-rollout/simulator-branching selection, test-time self-training); [R] = red line (R2R_test.json original instructions, test-scan human annotations, val/test for training). Main results. (1) In closed-loop VLN-CE the dominant compute-for-accuracy levers are TRAINING-side, not test-side: a clearance-aware privileged expert for DAgger plus a learned step-collision shield, HA-style long-instruction augmentation filtered by the navigator, and RL on the true challenge objective (success AND zero collisions). Evidence: on human-free R2R-CE under HA-VLN physics, ETP-R1's collision-free SR is 37.7 vs raw 64.6, a depth guard gives 50.1 and a navmesh-oracle guard 61.3 (vln-safe logs); RL adds +7.5 to +10.2 SR in SimpleVLN and +2.1 in ETP-R1; DAgger adds +3 to +5 SPL in VLN-CE and cuts HA-VLN-CMA TCR 47 -> 27; data scaling added +11 test SR (ScaleVLN) and +4-5 (SRDF's navigator-filtered flywheel). (2) NEW computation on vln-safe per-episode logs (613 R2R-CE val_unseen trajectories x 3 sibling instructions): for modern CE agents, sibling-instruction failures are strongly correlated (P(sibling fails | this fails) 0.50-0.63 vs base failure 0.33-0.44), so a 3-instruction majority proxy adds only +2.0 to +2.3 SR (ETP-R1 64.6->66.6, OmniNav 67.5->69.8, StreamVLN 56.0->58.2; oracle-guarded clean SR 61.3->63.6). B4's +6-7 SR instruction vote was measured on 2021 DISCRETE rankers; the CE analogue is about +2 to +5 AND it is [G] in the compliant track. (3) Agent ensembles are great proposers but naive voting buys nothing: union of ETP-R1/OmniNav/StreamVLN stops within 3 m on 87.2% of episodes, but the >=2-of-3 majority (67.1%) equals the best single agent (67.5%); 3 agents x 3 instructions (9 rollouts) give any-success 96.6% and >=4/9 79.1%, so a learned selector is what converts compute into SR, and selecting among complete rollouts is [G]. (4) The compliant test-time levers are: stop verification with return-to-best-visited-node (headroom = OSR-SR = 5.6 to 9.1 points across agents; NavGPT-3's frontier planner recovered +2.1/+4.6 SR over its 8B VLA), per-step policy ensembles (EnvEdit-style logit averaging; 2022 RxR-Habitat winner used a snapshot ensemble), instruction-side TTA (raw HA text + LLM-condensed R2R-style + sub-instruction list), and uncertainty-gated in-budget look-ahead (agents use a median of only 55-64 of 500 actions). Expected compliant stack: about +8 to +18 challenge-SR over a base agent with depth shield and HA-R2R SFT (non-additive, inference), i.e. roughly 0.50-0.62 SR on HA-R2R test (Score ~0.8 per SR point). Gray levers (sibling fusion, best-of-N rollouts) could add another +3 to +8 if the organizers allow them. Caveats: no new web search was possible; several literature magnitudes (EnvEdit ensemble gain, PREVALENT/Marky, SimpleVLN pass@k) are unverified and marked.

## New computation: how much redundancy do sibling instructions and agent ensembles really carry in VLN-CE?

R2R-CE val_unseen, 613 trajectories × 3 instructions (same start and goal), vln-safe per-episode logs. 'Majority proxy' = fraction of episodes on trajectories where ≥2 of 3 siblings succeed. It is a proxy for an endpoint vote; B4's medoid vote on discrete rankers added about +3.3 more on top of it.

| Agent / arm | SR (per episode) | 0/1/2/3 of 3 succeed (trajectories) | Majority proxy | Any-of-3 | P(sib fails \| fails) vs P(fail) | OSR − SR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ETP-R1 (slide, raw) | 0.646 | 95 / 110 / 146 / 262 | 0.666 (+2.0) | 0.845 | 0.607 vs 0.354 | +6.1 |
| OmniNav (slide, raw) | 0.675 | 58 / 127 / 170 / 258 | 0.698 (+2.3) | 0.905 | 0.503 vs 0.325 | +7.2 |
| StreamVLN (slide, raw) | 0.560 | 126 / 130 / 172 / 185 | 0.582 (+2.2) | 0.794 | 0.627 vs 0.440 | +8.9 |
| ETPNav (slide, raw) | 0.569 | 137 / 120 / 142 / 214 | 0.581 (+1.2) | 0.777 | – | +7.1 |
| ETP-R1 + depth guard, collision-free SR | 0.501 | 187 / 111 / 134 / 181 | 0.514 (+1.3) | 0.695 | – | +20.0* |
| ETP-R1 + navmesh-oracle guard, collision-free SR | 0.613 | 118 / 105 / 148 / 242 | 0.636 (+2.3) | 0.808 | 0.635 vs 0.374 (raw) | +6.9 |
| Airbert (discrete ranker, B4) | 0.687 | 72 / 144 / 232 / 335 | 0.724 (+3.7) | 0.908 | – | medoid vote 0.757 |
| VLN-BERT (discrete ranker, B4) | 0.593 | 145 / 154 / 214 / 270 | 0.618 (+2.5) | 0.815 | – | medoid vote 0.651 |

\* For guarded arms, OSR is measured on raw passes, so OSR − clean SR mixes stop errors with collisions.

Agent-level (same episode, 1,839 episodes): ETP-R1 + OmniNav + StreamVLN, raw: best single 0.675, ≥2-of-3 majority 0.671, union 0.872. Collision-free: 0.377 / 0.305 / 0.628. 9 rollouts per trajectory (3 agents × 3 instructions), raw: ≥5/9 0.679, ≥4/9 0.791, ≥3/9 0.874, any 0.966. Failures that stop within 5 m: 21–29% for CE agents (median failure NE 7.4–8.7 m), versus 38–41% for the discrete rankers. Actions used: median 55–64 of 500.

**Reading.** Sibling and agent failures are strongly correlated, because path and scene difficulty dominate. Plain voting therefore buys about +2 SR from siblings and nothing from agents. The value of extra rollouts lies in the union (87–97%), which only a good selector can reach, and selecting among rollouts is gray in the compliant track. The compliant analogue is the stop-headroom OSR − SR (6–9 points), which a verifier with return-to-best-visited-node can partly recover inside one run.

   _Evidence:_ b10_scripts/detect.py over vln-safe etpr1 arms; findings/B7 item 5

Test-time-compute agents need a patched local clock that emulates the scorer schedule (C1 schedule(S)) and clearance margins >= the p90 human motion extent (0.8 m).  
   _Evidence:_ CONTEXT.md fact 4; findings/C1-infra-and-replay-farm.md L41, L103

## Survey notes by sub-question

**(a) Data augmentation and pretraining.** Speaker back-translation (EnvDrop +4), PREVALENT (~1M speaker instructions, ~+3 test SR [memory]), ScaleVLN (+11 test from 1,200 scenes and 4.9M pairs), EnvEdit (style/object edits; a 3-agent ensemble is 'complementary' [gain unverified]), SRDF (navigator-filtered generator flywheel, ~85–86 val-unseen), ETP-R1 (Gemini-written instructions for PREVALENT paths), NavGPT VLA (19.28M examples → 76.94 on full R2R-CE). In continuous settings, augmentation without DAgger can hurt (VLN-CE CMA_Aug 0.19 vs CMA 0.22 SPL); with DAgger and a progress monitor it is the best configuration (0.30). For HA-VLN, the organizers' generation prompt is public, so HA-style rewriting of any augmentation pool is cheap. The binding constraints are human placement (61 train scans only), long context (≥256 tokens), and navigator filtering of hallucinated text.

**(b) Ensembling.** Per-step logit averaging (EnvEdit `ensemble_rollout`) and snapshot ensembles (2022 RxR-Habitat winner) are compliant single-run ensembles. Endpoint votes over instructions (B4: +6–7 on discrete rankers; CE estimate +2–5) and over agents (+0 naive; union +20) need sibling or multi-rollout permission. Majority voting over heterogeneous CE agents is worthless without a selector.

**(c) Test-time compute.** Measured CE evidence includes NavGPT-3's planner (+2.1 to +4.6 SR, with non-compliant plumbing), vln-safe's guards (+12 to +24 collision-free SR), and historical beam search (+11 to +19, physically traversed). Comparative judging beats absolute judging (C2Nav: 31 vs 12–28 SR). Process-reward analogues exist: progress monitors and FAST features (PM 50.1, fusion 64.0 vs oracle 90.2). General LLM results on self-consistency, trained verifiers and PRMs [memory] support spending test compute on verification rather than more samples. In the compliant regime, that means verification of stops and subgoals inside one run.

**Speaker scoring as a verifier.** A speaker or back-translation score is weak alone (FAST 43.7 top-1) but the best complement (+10.9 SR in Speaker-Follower; weight 0.94 in VLN-BERT's fusion). In the compliant track it can only score the executed trajectory prefix, not unseen candidate paths. Use it as one feature of the P4 verifier, and use PMI (log P(instr | path) − log P(instr)) to remove the length bias of 112-word texts.

## Implications

- In the compliant track the big gains are bought with TRAINING compute (clearance-aware DAgger, HA-style augmentation, RL on success AND zero collisions), not with test-time sampling. Test-time compute is worth a further +4 to +10 SR when spent on stop verification, per-step ensembles and instruction TTA.
- B4's '+6-7 SR from 3-instruction voting' does not transfer as-is: it was measured on 2021 discrete rankers in the privileged regime. Modern CE agents gain only about +2 (majority proxy) to +5 (medoid estimate), and sibling use is itself a gray question in the compliant track. Do not plan around it until the organizers rule.
- Agent ensembles are worth running for their diversity (union 87% vs best 67.5%), but only a trained selector or a per-step fusion with a verifier turns that diversity into SR; plain majority voting is worthless (67.1 vs 67.5).
- Because failures are strongly correlated within a trajectory (path difficulty), the cheapest diagnostic is per-trajectory: build a 'hard-path' bucket (0/3 siblings succeed: 9-22% of trajectories for the four strong agents) and target augmentation at it (stairs, multi-room, repeated rooms, long instructions).
- Stop errors are a bounded but real prize (OSR-SR 6-9 points). A compliant agent can revisit nodes it has seen, so return-to-best-node with a verifier is the most defensible test-time-compute lever and also an 'innovation' story (process-reward/verifier for embodied stopping).
- The 500-action budget leaves room (median 55-64 actions used) for uncertainty-gated look-ahead, but every extra metre is collision exposure and every rotation panorama costs ~24 actions; gating by verifier confidence is mandatory.
- Validation noise (~1.0-1.6 SR SE on val_unseen) means many +1-2 SR levers cannot be confirmed from one run; budget several seeds, pool val_seen, and keep a pre-registered decision log.
- Slow agents change what they see: wall-clock human animation means heavy per-step compute must be paired with a scorer-speed clock emulation in the local simulator and generous human clearance.

## Recommendations (actionable within 5 weeks)

- P1 [C-train] Clearance-aware privileged expert + learned shield (expected +5 to +12 challenge-SR over the same policy with an analytic depth guard; evidence: vln-safe 37.7 -> 50.1 -> 61.3, VLN-CE DAgger +3-5 SPL, HA-VLN CMA-DA TCR 47 -> 27; cost 6-10 eng-days, 100-400 GPU-h). Recompute each TRAIN scan's navmesh at radius 0.25-0.30 m over the union of all 120 human frames; replace ShortestPathFollower/GreedyGeodesicFollower in the StreamVLN/ETP-R1/HA-VLN DAgger collectors with this expert (no sliding steps; verify zero collisions on the official 0.1.7 navmesh); beta = p^k with p 0.5-0.75, 3-5 rounds; also train a step-collision predictor (depth/RGB -> 'try_step shortens the 0.25 m move') on millions of train-scan samples and gate every MOVE_FORWARD.
- P2 [C-train] HA-style long-instruction augmentation with a navigator filter (expected +2 to +6 over HA-R2R-train-only fine-tuning; evidence: EnvDrop BT +4, ScaleVLN +11, SRDF +4-5, HA-VLN 2.0 human-text ablation; cost 3-5 eng-days, ~$40 API or a few local GPU-hours for text, plus training). Rewrite R2R-CE train, EnvDrop-CE (157k), ScaleVLN-CE (~150k) and RxR-CE-en instructions with the organizers' published prompt (Data/HA-R2R/README.md), filling [start]/[during]/[end] from human_motion.json on the 61 TRAIN scans only (never the 11 val_unseen scans) and generic human clauses for HM3D/Gibson; generate 2-3 variants per path with different LLMs (Qwen/Llama/Claude) to mimic GPT-4o/LLaMA-3 mixture and its hallucinated distractors; keep pairs whose current policy rollout has nDTW >= 0.9 (SRDF filter); train with max instruction length >= 256 tokens; mix 1 HA-R2R batch : 3-9 augmented batches (ScaleVLN used 1:9).
- P3 [C-train] RL on the challenge objective (expected +2 to +8; evidence SimpleVLN +7.5-10.2, ETP-R1 +2.1; cost 8-12 eng-days, 300-1,000 GPU-h): GRPO with group size 8 and reward 1[success] * 1[zero collisions] (collision = any shortened step), dense shaping -lambda per collision step, on HA-R2R train + filtered augmentation, humans inserted, scorer-speed human clock.
- P4 [C] Stop verifier + return-to-best-visited-node (expected +2 to +4; headroom OSR-SR 6-9 points; NavGPT-3 planner +2.1/+4.6; cost 5-8 eng-days, ~100 GPU-h): fine-tune a Qwen3-VL-8B judge on (instruction, keyframes up to node i, endpoint views) -> P(goal within 3 m), trained on 50-100k rollouts of OUR policies on train episodes collected from scan-held-out folds (4 folds over 61 scans) so failure rates look like unseen scenes; features also from the policy (stop logit, progress estimate) and a speaker/PMI score; at each STOP compare the current node with visited nodes and navigate back via the agent's own depth map + GPS when the margin exceeds a threshold tuned on val_seen.
- P5 [C] Per-step policy ensemble (expected +1 to +3; evidence EnvEdit logit averaging, 2022 RxR-Habitat snapshot ensemble, partial failure decorrelation 0.49-0.62 vs 0.33-0.44; cost 2-4 eng-days, M x inference): average action/waypoint logits of 3-5 snapshots/seeds/augmentation variants of the SAME architecture; for heterogeneous agents (graph planner vs VLA chunks) vote on the next subgoal position (cluster medoid) and break ties with the P4 verifier; never use plain majority over final endpoints.
- P6 [C] Instruction-side TTA (expected +1 to +3, no direct evidence; cost 2-4 eng-days): train a 'de-HA' condenser on the 10,819 HA-R2R-train -> R2R-CE-train pairs; at inference feed the policy (a) the raw HA text, (b) the condensed R2R-style text, (c) an ordered sub-instruction list, and fuse per-step logits (or route each variant to the model fine-tuned on that style). Uses only the episode's own instruction, so it is unambiguously compliant.

## Flags and contradictions

- A6 recommendation 5 calls multi-rollout seed/policy selection 'compliant'. C3 classes simulator-branching selection as gray (T3: do not use unless allowed). This report follows C3.
- B4/B1 plan for 6 instructions per trajectory (3 HA + 3 original R2R). In the compliant track, the R2R originals are a red line (C3 T8) and the HA siblings are an open question (C3 Q4).
- B4's +6–7 SR instruction-vote figure applies to 2021 discrete rankers. For CE closed-loop agents the computed proxy is +2.0–2.3.
- NavGPT-3's +2.1/+4.6 SR planner gain was obtained with panoramic multi-view history, navmesh-derived labels and waypoint teleports. It is evidence for test-time compute, not a compliant number.

## Open questions

- Will the organizers allow (a) joint use of the 3 sibling instructions of a trajectory and (b) selecting among several local rollouts? These decide whether G1/G2 (+3 to +8 combined) are available.
- How diverse are HA-R2R TEST siblings? They are LLM expansions of 3 different R2R instructions with the SAME human annotations, so their redundancy may differ from R2R-CE (measure on HA-R2R val_unseen once the 0/1/2/3 histogram can be computed for our agents).
- SimpleVLN pass@k (K=4 default in its launcher) numbers are not in the README; the seed-level self-consistency headroom of a stochastic VLA (temperature 0.3) is unmeasured.
- Magnitude of per-step logit-averaging ensembles (EnvEdit, 2022 RxR-Habitat snapshot ensemble) was not verifiable offline; PREVALENT and RxR/Marky augmentation gains are from memory only.
- How much of the HA-style penalty is recovered by fine-tuning on HA-R2R train alone vs. by de-HA rewriting at test time vs. by augmentation? Needs a 3-arm experiment on val_unseen.
- Can a compliant learned step-collision predictor approach the navmesh-oracle guard (61.3 clean SR) or only the depth guard (50.1)? This is the largest uncertainty in the expected-gain stack.
- What scorer speed (steps/s) does CodaBench replay at, and therefore which human frames will the replay show? Needed to configure the local clock for slow test-time-compute agents.
- Is changing the single forward camera's resolution/HFOV allowed (SimpleVLN/StreamVLN trained at 640x480; official camera 224x224 HFOV 90)? Affects which ensembles are possible without retraining.
