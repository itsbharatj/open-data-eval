# W3 — Perception & instruction grounding (compliant inputs)

Goal: turn the 112-word instruction and the egocentric stream into a landmark program the policy can follow, with human
activities as anchors.

Instruction parser (LLM): extract an ordered program {start context, [turn/pass/enter/until landmark]*, stop condition}, the list
of human-activity mentions (role: start/during/end), and the end-region type (MP3D region vocabulary: bedroom, hallway, kitchen,
lounge, dining, bathroom, closet, office, stairs, entryway/foyer/lobby, balcony, porch/terrace, gym, laundry/mudroom, family
room, tv room, …). Instructions were generated by ChatGPT-4o/LLaMA-3 from the original R2R text plus [start]/[during]/[end]
human-activity slots that paraphrase annotated category strings like "hallway:An individual cleaning or vacuuming." — exploit this:
the end-slot human is usually at or near the goal region.

Perception per frame: person detection/segmentation (YOLO-seg or OWL-ViT, or a VLM), activity description of detected people
(VLM caption) to match against the instruction's mentions; room-type and salient-object tags (CLIP/VLM) for the semantic map;
door/opening detection from depth. Fuse into the topological memory (node = pose; attributes = tags, people seen with 3-D positions).

Evaluation (val_unseen, GT in `oracle/`): (a) parser accuracy on end-region and mention extraction (LLM-judged against GT path
region sequence from the MP3D .house file); (b) human-mention → detected-person matching precision/recall; (c) does the end
mention lie within 3 m of the GT goal (how often)? Target ≥ 0.8 for (b) and report (c).

Interfaces: `parse(instruction) -> program.json`; `perceive(frame, pose) -> {people:[{xyz, caption}], tags:[...], openings:[...]}`.
Report: tables for (a)–(c) with denominators; 20 qualitative failure cases.
